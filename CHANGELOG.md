- Ranked-row tooltips follow the profile and hit model on screen and can display saved stats while the client retrieves the item.
- Settings now appear at the top of Settings & Credits. The F1-Forever BiS tooltip line and weighted upgrade or downgrade comparisons have independent switches, both enabled by default.
